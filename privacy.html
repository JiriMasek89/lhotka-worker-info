Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026.

Lhotka Mac Worker is a personal-use application operated by its owner, Jiří Mašek. It is intended for the owner’s own project workflow.

Information processed

The application reads project input files, control documents, job metadata and processing receipts from the Google Drive account authorized by its owner. It generates drawings, validation reports and processing logs on the owner’s Mac and uploads output archives and status information to the configured Google Drive location.

Use and access

Data is used to execute and validate project jobs. Results are available to the owner and to the project’s configured coordinator through existing Google Drive permissions. The workflow may involve ChatGPT as coordinator. Its processing is governed by the owner’s account settings and the relevant provider’s policies. This site does not publish project data.

Credentials and storage

OAuth credentials are stored locally outside the code repository. Local inputs, outputs, logs and job history may remain on the Mac for recovery and verification. Uploaded results remain in Google Drive until removed by the owner. The application has no automatic retention deadline.

Owner controls

The owner can stop the worker, revoke its access through Google Account permissions, and remove local or Drive files. Revoking access does not itself delete existing copies. Project data is not sold or used for advertising by the worker.

This website

The static site contains no application analytics, forms, login or embedded third-party scripts. If hosted on GitHub Pages, the hosting provider may process ordinary web request information under its own privacy policy.

Contact

Contact the application owner through the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
